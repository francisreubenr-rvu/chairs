Welcome to Chairs

You are looking at the whole training programme in one place: six modules, 116 slides, covering the Rules of Procedure, Effective Communication, Design Thinking, Entrepreneurship & Innovation, the Major Events that shaped the world you will debate, and how to research a company portfolio to primary-source standard.

Two things worth knowing before you start. First, every line is tagged with where it came from: a crimson tag means it traces to a Diplomathon document at the page named, and in Module 1 those are binding rules. A green tag means it is added teaching, useful but not procedure. You should always be able to tell the difference between what the rules say and what anyone, including me, thinks about them.

Second, this is built to be revised alone, not only watched once:

  • Your place is saved. Close the tab and it offers to bring you back.
  • It works offline after your first visit.
  • Your progress is tracked. Each module shows how many slides you have visited.
  • / searches all 116 slides.
  • M jumps between modules.
  • G opens a grid of every slide so you can see the whole deck and jump anywhere.
  • P prints the deck as study notes, one slide per page.
  • T changes theme, F goes fullscreen, ? lists every key.
  • Any slide is linkable: send a classmate the URL.

The committee rewards the delegate who can make a claim, name its source, propose an action, and defend all three under challenge. Everything here is aimed at that.
