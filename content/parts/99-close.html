Putting it together Trainer plan

How the six modules meet in one speech

Ninety seconds on the Speakers List uses all six at once. That is the point of the programme.

ModuleWhat it contributes to those 90 seconds
01 · Rules of ProcedureThat you get 90 seconds at all, what must be in them, and how to yield the remainder
02 · CommunicationThe structure and delivery that make the content land, and the listening that lets you answer
03 · Design ThinkingThe defined problem behind your POV, and the tested solution you propose
04 · Entrepreneurship & InnovationWhether that solution is feasible, who funds it, and how it grows
05 · Major EventsThe past of the agenda, and the precedent your argument rests on
06 · Company PortfolioThe primary source behind every claim you make
The single test of the whole programme: can you make a claim, name its source, propose an action, and defend all three under a Challenge?
The order you will actually use them in Before conference: Module 6 gathers the evidence, Module 5 supplies the precedent, Module 3 defines the problem, Module 4 tests the solution. In the room: Module 1 gives you the procedure and Module 2 gives you the delivery. Research is slow and front-loaded; performance is fast and unrepeatable.
