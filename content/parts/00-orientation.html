The research spine ROP p.3 md §2

One five-step spine runs through every module

The Rules of Procedure name five research points. Every other module is a way of doing one of them better.

  1. Portfolio. Who you are: Country, Character or CompanyModule 6 covers the Company case in full
  2. Committee: the organisation you represent that portfolio inWhat it regulates, and why your portfolio sits in the room
  3. Agenda: past of the agenda, and current situation of the agendaModule 5 supplies the historical half of this
  4. Policy stance: PEST analysis against the comparators that matterModule 3 turns the stance into a defined problem
  5. Possible solutions: what you will actually proposeModule 4 pressure-tests it for feasibility; Module 2 delivers it
Nothing on this spine is optional. Your first speech under the Speakers List must carry two of these five aloud: your Point of View on the Agenda, and your Solution on the Agenda.
